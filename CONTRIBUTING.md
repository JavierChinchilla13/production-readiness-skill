# Contributing

Thanks for helping. The most useful contributions are:

- **Corrections of outdated facts** (a plan limit, a console screen, a legal reference). Include a source and the date you checked it, and update the "Last verified" line.
- **New entries** for `07-decisions-not-taken.md` (with the trigger to revisit) and `08-mistakes-we-made.md` (symptom, cause, fix).
- Stack variants (other hosts, databases, CDNs) as separate reference files or sections.
- Jurisdiction notes, clearly labelled with the country and the date, and always pointing to a professional review.

## Rules

- **No secrets, personal data, client names, real domains or copyrighted material.** Use `example.com`, documentation IP ranges and placeholders.
- **No claims of legal compliance** and no copied legal text. Describe approaches and what to ask a professional.
- Keep the tone practical: symptom → cause → fix; decision → reason → when to revisit.
- Facts must be verifiable. If you could not verify something, say "not verified".
- Prefer small pull requests, one topic each.
