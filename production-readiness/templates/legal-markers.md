# Marking drafts: `[PLACEHOLDER]` and `[PROPOSAL]`

Two visible markers keep drafts honest and make the professional's review fast.

| Marker | Meaning | Who fills it |
|---|---|---|
| `[PLACEHOLDER: legal name of the owner]` | A fact only the owner can supply | The owner |
| `[PROPOSAL: respond to requests within 5 business days]` | A value the developer proposes, with a technical reason, to be confirmed | A qualified professional |

Rules

- Style them so they are distinguishable **without relying on colour** (different word, different underline).
- Never fill a `[PLACEHOLDER]` with invented data.
- Every `[PROPOSAL]` has a justification recorded in a research note (what the code does, which source supports it, what could not be verified).
- Keep a banner on each legal page: "Draft pending legal review", plus a version string (for example `2026-11-draft-1`) that is stored with each acceptance.
- Keep a list of all open markers; the launch checklist requires zero `[PLACEHOLDER]` and all `[PROPOSAL]` reviewed.
- Typical placeholders: legal name and ID of the owner, public contact address, governing law and venue, supervisory authority, provider regions and policy links, dates of effect, image/font credits.
- Typical proposals: retention periods, response time to rights requests, session length, incident notice time, what is kept de-identified after deletion, minimum age and guardian authorisation band.
